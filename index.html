Biography

Filmmaker · Tehran, Iran

Behnam
Khosravi

Visual Storyteller & Director

Behnam Khosravi is an Iranian filmmaker whose work explores the intersection of emotion, memory, and human identity. Through carefully composed frames and intimate narratives, he crafts cinematic experiences that linger long after the screen fades to black.

His films — marked by their use of natural light, contemplative pacing, and visual poetry — reflect a deeply personal approach to storytelling. He is also the author of two books that bridge the worlds of cinema and literature.
