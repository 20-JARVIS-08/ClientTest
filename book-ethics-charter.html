About the Book

At a time when the world is grappling with deep moral crises and profound political challenges, The Charter of Human Ethics, a thought-provoking work by Behnam Khosravi, rises as an emerging manifesto to save human societies from decay. It is a bold legal-political work that, with a structure-challenging perspective, confronts the very concepts that govern the current world order.

Why Should Ethics Take Precedence Over Law?

In this work, Behnam Khosravi raises a fundamental question: can law, without ethics, truly guarantee human wellbeing? The author's answer in The Charter of Human Ethics is unequivocal: "No."

He argues that human rights are only effective when built upon the firm pillars of ethics. Through precise legal and political reasoning, Khosravi proposes that governments move beyond existing conventions and adopt the Charter of Human Ethics as a foundational document that takes precedence over all current legal frameworks.

Key Themes of the Book

01

The Primacy of Ethics Over Law

A critique of current legal systems and an account of why politics must be reconnected with moral virtue.

02

Human Dignity as a Founding Principle

The Charter insists that no political expediency should ever outweigh the inherent dignity of the human being.

03

Practical Pathways for Governments

Far from a purely theoretical work, the book offers states and leaders a roadmap for institutionalizing ethics to avert moral and political collapse.

04

Lasting Peace Through Ethics

An argument that genuine global peace is achieved through respect for human dignity, not through fragile military and political treaties.

"If human rights are the spine of the global order, ethics is the spirit that must be breathed into that body — without it, nothing remains but a lifeless structure."
