Biography

Behnam
Khosravi

Filmmaker & Visual Storyteller

Behnam Khosravi is an Iranian filmmaker and visual artist whose work explores the intersection of emotion, memory, and human identity. Through carefully composed frames and intimate narratives, he crafts cinematic experiences that linger long after the screen fades to black.

Drawn to cinema from an early age, Behnam developed his craft through years of study and hands-on production experience — directing, writing, and collaborating on short films that have been recognized for their distinct visual poetry and emotional depth.

His most recent project, Mandala, reflects his ongoing exploration of cyclical human experience — the patterns we repeat, the memories that shape us, and the quiet moments between certainty and doubt.

Cinema is not about what you show — it's about what you make the audience feel in the silence between frames. — Behnam Khosravi
