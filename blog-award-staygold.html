Award from Stay Gold

Mandala Shines Again: a second international award for Behnam Khosravi's short film, this time from the Stay Gold Film Festival.

MANDALA's fourth international festival appearance came with an award — a jury recognition for its narrative and artistic quality.

Mandala Shines Again: a second international award for Behnam Khosravi's short film. "Mandala," written and directed by Behnam Khosravi, has won the Golden Standard Award at the 4th edition of the Stay Gold Film Festival — the film's fourth international festival appearance. The film screened in the festival's official selection on June 28, 2026, with final results and winners announced on July 4, 2026.

What the jury said

The jury recognized "Mandala" for its powerful storytelling, artistic quality, creativity, and the high standards of independent filmmaking it represents. In its official announcement, the Stay Gold Film Festival congratulated Behnam Khosravi, noting that "Mandala" impressed the jury with its outstanding narrative, artistic quality, and creative vision — earning recognition as one of the standout works of this edition.
