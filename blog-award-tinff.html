MANDALA Nominated at TINFF
In Two Categories

MANDALA has received two nominations at the 10th Toronto International Nollywood Film Festival (TINFF).

MANDALA's international journey has now reached Toronto, Canada — a double nomination that marks the film's latest milestone on the festival circuit.

The short film MANDALA, directed by Behnam Khosravi, has received two nominations at the 10th Toronto International Nollywood Film Festival (TINFF), in the following categories:

· Best Experimental Student Short
· Best Narrative Short Experimental

The film will be screened on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, at 4:31 PM at the Fairview Library Theatre in Toronto, Canada, followed by a Q&A session with the filmmakers.

About TINFF

TINFF is one of Canada's respected international film festivals, recognized by the Canadian Screen Awards academy, with selected works positioned for consideration at the country's leading film events. Screening works from dozens of countries, the festival is a major event for independent and international cinema in Canada.

International Distribution

The film's distribution is handled by Seven Skies. For more information about MANDALA's screenings and international distribution, please contact the company via email at distribution@sevenskies.co.
