MANDALA Heads to SEAIFF
A Fifth International Appearance

The short film MANDALA has been officially selected for the 4th Southeast Asia International Film Festival in Pattaya, Thailand.

MANDALA's international journey now extends to Southeast Asia — a fresh selection that broadens the short film's global reach even further.

The short film MANDALA, directed by Behnam Khosravi, has been officially selected for the 4th Southeast Asia International Film Festival (SEAIFF) in its latest international appearance. The festival is held in Pattaya, Thailand, and aims to showcase independent works from around the world, hosting filmmakers from a wide range of countries.

The 4th edition of SEAIFF will take place on December 12, 2026, and beyond film screenings, it offers filmmakers opportunities for networking and international collaboration. This selection marks MANDALA's fifth international festival appearance, following the Accolade Global Film Competition, the Pune Film Festival in India, and the Stay Gold Film Festival.

Distribution

International distribution of the film is handled by Seven Skies. For screening and distribution inquiries, the company can be reached at distribution@sevenskies.co.
