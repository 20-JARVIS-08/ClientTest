Biography

Director · Tehran, Iran
Visual Storyteller & Director

Behnam Khosravi is an Iranian director whose work explores the intersection of emotion, memory, and human identity. Through carefully composed frames and intimate narratives, he crafts cinematic His films — marked by their use of natural light, contemplative pacing, and visual poetry — reflect a deeply personal approach to storytelling. He is also the author of two books that bridge the worlds of cinema and literature.
