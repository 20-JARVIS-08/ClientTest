MANDALA Heads to Canada's MISAFF
Film Festival

The short film MANDALA, directed by Behnam Khosravi, has been officially selected for MISAFF in Canada — the film's 8th international festival appearance.

MANDALA's international run continues in Canada, with an official selection at MISAFF arriving just weeks after the film's Best Experimental Short win at TINFF in Toronto.

The short film MANDALA, directed by Behnam Khosravi, has been selected for the official section of MISAFF (Mosaic International South Asian Film Festival) in Canada. MISAFF is recognized on the list of festivals qualifying for the Canadian Screen Awards.

MANDALA previously screened at the 10th edition of the TINFF (Toronto International Nollywood Film Festival), where it won the award for Best Experimental Short Film. TINFF is also a qualifying festival for the Canadian Screen Awards and Telefilm Canada. Counting this latest selection, MANDALA has now screened at a total of 8 international festivals.

About MISAFF

MISAFF is a Canadian film festival centered on mosaic and South Asian cinema, bringing together independent filmmakers from around the world. Its Canadian Screen Awards qualifying status adds further credibility to the films selected for its program.
